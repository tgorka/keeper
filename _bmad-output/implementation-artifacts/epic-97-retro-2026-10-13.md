# Epic 97 retrospective — A voice that takes turns (2026-10-13)

Three stories, built as six rungs where the plan had two. All six PRs are open in GitHub stack #486 (#459, #461, #462, #489, #490, #491), none merged, and CI went green on all six after flaky jobs were rerun (stack-notes 2026-10-12). GPT-6 Astra reviewed rungs 1–3 on 2026-10-05 against Claude Opus 5.5 authors; Claude Fable 5.1 reviewed rungs 4–6 on 2026-10-11, also against Opus 5.5, and because both are Anthropic models those three reviews are not fully model-independent. Every story is at `review` and the epic is `in-progress`: nothing has run on a Mac or an iPhone with the turn models yet, and no artifact records the models added to the owner's `_models/`.

Final shas are the pushed ones (stack-notes 2026-10-11, F9697b "FINAL"). Gates and proofs are taken from each rung's As built section in `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epic-97-a-voice-that-takes-turns.md` and from the lane reports named.

### 97.3 — "Mhm" is not an interruption, and the agent knows where you stopped it

- **Rung 4 `agents-97-heard` — #489, open, final `860b3a64`.**
  - **What you get:** when you stop an answer, the agent's next turn reads that answer cut where you stopped listening, with *[The person stopped listening here.]*. The host counts the cut itself. It takes the mark only from your own signed device, logs it once, and starts no turn.
  - **Proved:**
    - #7: `rooms::tests::a_heard_mark_counts_only_from_the_persons_signed_device_in_its_rooms`, the runtime test and the `agent_turns` test. Live on delectra's Synapse, `heard_event_is_validated_and_logged_once` passed in 88.9 s, covering another user, an unsigned login, a mark of the question, a second mark and a restart (`t-97heard-live.log`).
    - #8: `replay_truncates_at_heard_until`. Both replays agree.
    - Mutation: 25 mutants. M15 was a dead loop and was removed; M21 is equivalent (report-97heard.md).
  - **Owed:** nothing on a device for this rung alone. The mark is first sent by rung 5.
- **Rung 5 `agents-97-pause` — #490, open, final `394becae`.**
  - **What you get:** talking over an answer pauses it mid-word.
    - "mhm", "yeah" or "okay" in English, or "mhm", "tak", "no" or "jasne" in Polish, resumes it.
    - The stop phrase ends it.
    - Anything else stops it and becomes your next question.
    - Before that question goes in, your device tells the agent the last sentence it finished saying aloud.
  - **Proved:**
    - #1–#6 and #10 by tests: `voice_barge_in_pauses_speaking_before_anything_else` (one of the three stop-first tests, rewritten), `voice_paused_utterance_decides` (every branch, in English and Polish), `backchannel_list_is_per_language`, `voice_backchannel_without_models_uses_the_list_only`, `segmenter_reports_sentence_ends`, `heard_until_counts_finished_sentences` and `pause_timings_record_and_measure`.
    - Live on Synapse: the answer arrives `Anchored`, then as sentences with their ends 105 and 189 (`t-97pause-live.log`).
    - Mutation: 37 of 37 mutants killed.
    - On hesperia, workspace clippy passed and the shell crate's 537 tests passed (`mac-97pause.log`; three clean reruns after the fix round, `mac-97pause-fix1-rerun.log`).
  - **Owed:**
    - #11, the device run on hesperia and kalypso. It is also the only proof of the fix round's three shell fixes made by inspection (R326 (02)–(04)) and of DW-1216–DW-1218.
    - `install:ios`, which puts the synthesiser delegate and pause/continue on the phone for the first time.
- **Rung 6 `agents-97-mark` — #491, open, final `333c06bc`.**
  - **What you get:** in the agent room (the conversation pane and the notes view's dock), a stopped answer shows a thin rule, *You stopped listening here*, after the last sentence you heard, with the rest dimmed. It is drawn only from your own sealed mark and counted in Rust.
  - **Proved:**
    - #9: `the_room_folds_the_persons_own_sealed_marks`, `a_mark_splits_the_body_in_utf16_where_the_heard_sentences_end`, the room filter test, and the vitest suites for the bubble, the timeline store and the conversation pane.
    - Live, in a real encrypted room: `the_persons_heard_mark_is_drawn_beside_the_stream` gave `splitUtf16: 6`.
    - Browser: measured at 1280 px, 420 px and in the dock (report-97mark.md § Browser findings).
    - Mutation: 16 of 16 killed, plus the fix round's X01–X03 and G01–G03 (report-fix-9697.md).
  - **Owed:** seeing the rule live, during #11's "stop" step in the installed app. That is also the first WebKit rendering of it (report-97mark.md § Owed).

## What the independent reviews caught

**Rung 1 — `review-epic97-r1.md` (GPT-6 Astra, as the coordinator records it; the file does not name its model).** Round 1 asked for changes; no second round is recorded. Every finding was fixed in place in the rung. The rung's As built cites each one by the review's own id, and there is no R-line in the decisions file.
- **R97-01 (blocker for distribution):** ONNX Runtime's notices did not travel with the apps. Fixed: `licenses/`, `NOTICE`, both bundle configurations, and the bundle guard. DW-493 opened and closed 2026-10-05.
- **R97-02 (major):** the loader ignored the account and current-generation rule that the status line enforced. Fixed: `TurnDisk::load` decides; the models unload when the account is forgotten.
- **R97-03 (major):** a picked turn model sat in both hydration groups, so it could stop transcription. Fixed: the transcription group excludes any folder holding a `model.onnx`.
- **R97-04 (major):** switching a turn-model version invalidated transcription through the shared manifest. Fixed: each group digests only its own `models.toml` sections (`HydrateGroup.manifest`).
- **R97-05 (major):** an ONNX graph with other output names could panic the worker and leave the VAD waiting forever. Fixed: `TurnGraph` refuses at load, and a stopped worker is an error.
- **R97-06 (major):** concurrent loads could publish an older generation last. Fixed: `LoadSlot` publishes only the newest.
- **Minors:** R97-07 (the iOS cfg claimed an Intel simulator) and R97-08 (a refused pick sent you to controls that do not exist) were both fixed. The cfg is now arm64 iPhone only; the refusal names the key and the way out, with DW-490 for the missing picker.

**Rung 2 — `review-epic97-r2.md` (GPT-6 Astra, as recorded).** Round 1 asked for changes, and the review left severity unassigned. All three findings were ruled by **R173** (the line is pushed, not polled) and fixed in place.
- **R97-R2-01:** an older poll reply could overwrite newer settings and outlive teardown. R173: the reads are ticketed, so a reply older than the newest VM is dropped.
- **R97-R2-02:** an update was seen only if a read happened to catch `fetching`. R173: the shell announces `keeper://voice-wake` whenever a fact moves.
- **R97-R2-03:** each component polled for itself, even while hidden. R173: one listener for every host; nothing is held while folded.

**Rung 3 — `review-epic97-r3.md` (GPT-6 Astra, as recorded).** Round 1: changes required ("story 97.2 is not ready for acceptance"), severity unassigned. All ten findings were fixed in the rung (epic §97.2, *As built … review fixes R97-R3-01…10*); there is no R-line.
- **R97-R3-01:** in-flight audio or a model result could finish a different turn. Fixed: a listening generation fences every buffer and event.
- **R97-R3-02:** the listener's events raced, which could corrupt the timing record. Fixed: one ordered consumer, applied under one lock. DW-496 now keeps only the frame-time half.
- **R97-R3-03:** the audio tap could block on a mutex. Fixed: `try_lock`; a buffer that cannot be placed is dropped and counted.
- **R97-R3-04:** the hangover was 192 ms, not 224 ms. Fixed: the comparison now counts elapsed frames.
- **R97-R3-05:** `finish_recognition_ms` measured enqueueing, not `endAudio`. Fixed: the port reports `AudioEnded`. DW-497 opened.
- **R97-R3-06:** routine request rolling could cancel the recogniser inside the 600 ms final-words wait. Fixed: `voice::request::rolls`.
- **R97-R3-07:** a stale silence timer could skip the final-words wait. Fixed: the timer checks and spends in one hold.
- **R97-R3-08:** the parity test accepted NaN. Fixed: `smart_turn_parity_refuses_non_finite_bins`.
- **R97-R3-09:** the fixture generator copied Smart Turn code while the provenance said it copied none. Fixed: its own `last_eight_seconds`.
- **R97-R3-10:** the macOS unsafe inventory miscounted. Fixed: 24, with `default_input_device_name`.

## What to keep

- **The epic's "failure shape" list as the reviewer's blocker checklist.** Each 10-11 review checked it by name: no `StopSpeaking` before `PauseSpeaking`, a resume only under the backchannel rule, and `heard_until` never past a finished sentence (review-97pause-1.md § Verdict; review-97heard-1.md; review-97mark-1.md § Failure shapes).
- **Count on the host and in Rust; never trust the device's number.**
  - The host recounts `heard_until` from `sentence` (R317).
  - The room recounts the split in Rust, in UTF-16, and the front counts nothing (rung 6).
  - That is why the host's fix round needed no change on the device (review-97pause-1.md: "neither host fix changes anything here").
- **Prove each fix with its own pre-fix mutant.** F1/F2 (heard), F1/F2/F4 (pause) and X01 (mark) are the old shape, and each is killed by the new test. Equivalent mutants were argued and probed, not pinned by an incidental test (M16's bit-identical probe, M21), and dead code was removed (M15).
- **Mutants from dangling commits on delectra.** The working tree was never mutated (report-97heard.md, report-97heard-fix1.md). The in-tree runs are the ones that left mutant text behind.
- **Live tests against delectra's Synapse for the Matrix-shaped risks:** a forged anchor, another user, an unsigned login, a redelivery, a restart, and a real encrypted room's fold (`t-97heard-live.log`, `t-97pause-live.log`, `t-97mark-live.log`).
- **Small single-concern rungs with a written handoff.** The plan's one `epic97-turns` rung became four reviewable rungs. Each lane started from the previous lane's `signatures_for_*` section (report-97-restack.md, report-97heard.md, report-97pause.md), which is what the artifact-only rule needs.
- **Browser proof measured, not eyeballed:** rule and label widths at 1280 px, 420 px and in the dock, the accessibility reading order, and the console checked. The desktop token was never printed (report-97mark.md § Browser findings).
- **`cargo nextest` with a raised fd limit on hesperia, and rerere off for cascades** (report-restack-96tail-97.md).
