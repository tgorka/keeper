# Epic 98 retrospective — Your phone and your tablet (2026-10-13)

Four stories were built on 2026-10-11/12 as ten rungs on `agents-97-mark` (`333c06bc`): PRs #492–#501, all open in GitHub stack #486, none merged. CI is green after flake reruns, and these PRs carried keeper's first CI compile of the iOS shell (#492–#497) and its first Android build (#498) (stack-notes 2026-10-12).
All four stories are `review`, held by device runs on kalypso and the owner's Android tablet; 98.1 also waits for the owner's paid Apple enrolment.
