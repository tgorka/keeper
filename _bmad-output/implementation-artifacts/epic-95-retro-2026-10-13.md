### 95.1 — Memory files and the journal/proposal tools

- **`agents-95-memory`: #474, merged, final `1caff43e`** (on `83a2d3a1`, `agents-94-helpers`).
  - **What a person gets:** an agent keeps a daily journal and stages proposals for its `USER.md`, `MEMORY.md` and skills, but it can never change its core memory itself.
    - Every proposal is scanned with Hermes' threat patterns before it is written.
    - A proposal wider than the session's label is refused.
    - An entry a person wrote that carries an injection is shown as `[BLOCKED: …]` in the session's snapshot, and the file stays byte-identical.
    - After 10 person turns or 15 tool rounds, a review pass may propose, never write.
  - **Proved:** acceptance 1–12, each by a named test (epic § 95.1 *As built*, "Acceptance → proof"). The review fixes' 48 mutants were all killed (`mut95mem3.py`, `mut-95mem-summary3.log`).
  - **Built differently from the plan:**
    - The nudge's review pass is its own model run, not 94.4's `helper` (R126), and it spends the turn's one token budget (R226).
    - The journal does not use 89.5's `append_line` (R125).
    - Hermes' prompts are adapted, not verbatim.
  - **Owed:** no device run, because the rung has no shell code. DW-562 (no turn-level test of a review pass that loses its claim) and DW-575…DW-578 are open.

### 95.2 — The nightly consolidation

- **`agents-95-consolidate`: #475, merged, final `419c9a30`** (on `1caff43e`).
  - **What a person gets:** once a night (03:00, host time), an always-on host promotes proposals that came up in three sessions on three days, through OpenClaw's gates. Each agent's promotions are one commit that names its source sessions (`Memory-Origin`, `Source-Session` trailers). These changes wait for the person on a T2 review card instead of applying:
    - the agent's own repetition with no `owner`/`peer` input behind it;
    - any change on a shared drive;
    - a change that would lose more than a quarter of the file.

    A person's edit is never written over: guards compare git blob ids at the fetched head. A crash mid-commit rolls forward; what recovery cannot attribute holds the folder until the person settles it (DW-970).
  - **Proved:**
    - Acceptance 1–13 by named tests (epic § 95.2 *As built*, 2026-10-06).
    - #8's live lease test, `one_consolidator_per_drive_on_synapse`, passed on delectra's Synapse on 2026-10-06. In round 3 it passed again with a night and a curator racing the one maintenance claim.
    - Nine rounds of mutation, each survivor killed by a new test or recorded (`mut-95cons-3.log` … `mut-95cons-13-*.log`).
    - The macOS build of the recovery code is covered by CI486's hesperia run at the stack tip: keeper-sync, keeper-core and keeper-agent, 6514/6514 (report-ci486.md).
  - **Built differently from the plan:**
    - The review path is the host actions `memory_apply`/`skill_apply` at a fixed T2 (R128).
    - The night reads through `sync_once`, not `pull_now` (R205).
    - `Engine::commit_paths` became a roll-forward transaction with a durable intent (R207). It fails closed on what it cannot attribute (R250, R264, R268).
    - The curator shares one maintenance claim per drive (R207).
  - **Owed:**
    - both operator-verified items on electra (epic § 95.2);
    - a whole agentd night (DW-709);
    - a rerun of the live lease test after rounds 4–9 (report-95cons-restack.json).

### 95.3 — The weekly curator for skills

- **`agents-95-curate`: #476, merged, final `c57046b8`** (on `419c9a30`).
  - **What a person gets:** on Sundays at 04:00, an always-on host deals with each skill an agent made and no person has adopted:
    - after 14 days unchanged it is marked stale;
    - after 30 days it is moved to `_skills/.archive/`. It is never deleted, and every byte is kept (`R100` renames).

    The curator leaves these alone: a person's skill, an adopted, pinned or named one, and one whose ownership metadata it cannot read (R251). Proposals that came from a gate expire unread at 30 days.
  - **Proved:** acceptance 1–8 (epic § 95.3 *As built*). Mutation logs: `mut-95cur-6.log` … `mut-95cur-10.log`.
  - **Built differently from the plan:**
    - A skill's clock comes from git alone; AD-402's "reads skill use from the logs" was corrected.
    - There is no lease of its own: the curator uses the drive's one maintenance claim (R207, R208).
    - It reads protection from git's objects at one commit.
  - **Owed:** a live multi-host curator week (report-95cur-fix.json). DW-1070 is open.

### 95.4 — `drive_search` over the drives' OKF bundles

- **`agents-95-search`: #477, merged, final `8d8639c1`** (on `c57046b8`).
  - **What a person gets:** an agent can search the drives in its session's scope, through each drive's OKF bundles.
    - It never opens what a bundle excludes, a session's `workspace/`, `.keeper/` or `.git/`, and never reads an LFS pointer as text.
    - Queries are literal terms. It returns at most 25 hits, each labelled with its drive's readers.
    - It stops within 2 000 files, 16 MiB and 1.5 s, and says that it stopped.
    - A local-only session never sends its query to a remote embeddings model.
    - It writes nothing (R211).
    - A helper can search as its session does (R280).
  - **Proved:** acceptance 1–12. #1's real-drive parity test, `okf_matching_matches_the_drives_own_tools`, is `#[ignore]` because OA-95-2 is open (DW-713). It passed on 2026-10-06, in round 4 and after the restack (`gate-95srch-rs2-ported-ignored.log`).
  - **Built differently from the plan:**
    - `keeper-ported::okf` is written from the drive's documentation, not from its scripts (R137).
    - Labels follow R133.
    - One resolver is shared with 95.5 (R288).
  - **Owed:** a run on a real Mac volume for the dataless and case-folding halves (DW-715, DW-830; report-95srch-restack.json).

### 95.5 — From session to knowledge

- **`agents-95-knowledge`: #480, open, final `827428c0`.** The local final was `fe760de0` on `8d8639c1`. The 2026-10-11 cascade put it on `agents-ci-green` as `8a6c0342`, and CI486 rebased it unchanged to `827428c0` (report-ci486.md).
  - **What a person gets:** a steward's harvest turn writes candidate notes of at most 64 KiB into its own harvest session.
    - Each note is stamped `generated: agent:<agent>@<host>` and `human_reviewed: false`, and never carries `verified`.
    - Promoting a note into the drive's notes vault is the person's review of exactly the version they read. Ticking and unticking on the vault copy are guarded.
    - Promotions are checked, journaled copies that a crash can resume.
    - A promotion is refused when its audience is wider than the session's label, and when it would land where maintenance commits (R291).
  - **Proved:** acceptance #1–#7 by Rust tests (epic § 95.5 *As built*, Rust half). The mutation rerun after the restack killed 104 mutants (stack-notes 2026-10-10).
  - **Shell commands:** edited by inspection; CI's Tauri build (macOS) is green on stack #486 (stack-notes 2026-10-11 ~09:50).
  - **Owed:**
    - the hesperia operator-verified run;
    - the shell crate's tests on macOS, including `two_amendments_of_a_closed_note_both_land` and its lock-removal mutant. They never ran: hesperia was full, with ENOSPC on 2026-10-10 (report-95know-fix9.json).
- **`agents-95-panel`: #481, open, final `4bd7e9c0`.** The local final was `1c79e205`. The cascade made it `e7b3f22f`, and CI486 made it `4bd7e9c0` with a biome reformat of one test file.
  - **What a person gets:** in a session's detail, **Promote…** opens a panel. It lists:
    - each `## Promote` row with its state: in order, *Newer here*, *Source gone* (quiet), *Target missing* (loud), or unreadable (shown verbatim);
    - unlisted workspace files, one action away from promotion;
    - harvested notes, in their own group.

    A note opens whole, up to 64 KiB, and promoting it to notes is the person's *Reviewed by me*. Archiving a session needs a decision on every row and file, and removes only what was decided.
  - **Proved:** acceptance #8, by:
    - vitest through the real typed client over the mock shell;
    - Linux Chromium at 1280 px and 420 px;
    - Rust↔mock vectors (`promote-vectors.json`, `command-vectors.json`);
    - mutation in every round (epic § 95.5, panel *As built*).
  - **Owed:**
    - real WKWebView on hesperia (DW-780);
    - live drive runs (DW-785, DW-911);
    - the phone, which has no Sessions route (DW-781);
    - an independent review of the original UI (AI-95-8).
