## The one sentence

**A chip that deletes itself on the click meant to flip it, a bar in two pieces the owner wants in one, a wrapped line that starts under a glyph, a clear where a reset belongs, a minus you cannot see, five browser checkboxes, a rail that ignores the drives the list searches, captions and glyphs that say the wrong thing, a title that hides its path, a row tag that acts before you choose, a folder you cannot name, a history you cannot find and a note that leaves its space: the chip's sign becomes the control, the bar becomes one line that wraps, and the rail follows the drives.**

## Decisions this epic takes

- **AD-287 — The chip's sign is a control; the chip's body is not.** **Binds:** FR-616, FR-617, NFR-83; Story 79.1; UX-DR107. **Prevents:** the third click that deletes (`CYCLE` `notes-filters.ts:134` reaches `off` from `exclude`, and `cycleTag` `:453-460` is what the body calls at `note-filter-bar.tsx:80`); a second grammar for the tree or the editors (`tag-tree.tsx:142`, `space-editor.tsx:841`, `session-space-editor.tsx:826` all read `nextTagChipState`); a label that looks clickable and is; a compatibility path that keeps the cycle alive under a flag. **Rule:** Reversal of AD-273's coordinator resolution, on the owner's word and with its reason recorded in the table above. A chip is `[sign][label][×]`: the sign is a button that flips include ↔ exclude and never reaches *off*; the label is a span with no role, handler or Tab stop; × is the one removal. `notes-filters.ts` replaces `cycleTag`, `CYCLE` and `nextTagChipState` with `flipTag(tag)` and an exported `flipTagTerm(term)` (`include` → `exclude`, `exclude` → `include`, `off` → `include`, the last for the tree's first press); `removeTag` (`:462`) and `setTagTerm` (`:461`) are unchanged and remain the only other mutations. The sign's accessible name says what pressing it does — `Exclude tag client` while included, `Include tag client` while excluded — and its description carries the current polarity; × keeps `Clear tag client filter`. The tree (`tag-tree.tsx:103-156`) keeps its plain-press-replaces / shift-press-adds distinction for an *off* node and renders the split grammar once a node is active; the editors' draft state uses `flipTagTerm` where they used `nextTagChipState`. The five test files that pinned the cycle are retargeted to the new contract, never re-pinned to new strings.

- **AD-288 — One line, one row of controls.** **Binds:** FR-619, FR-620, FR-621, FR-623, NFR-80; Stories 79.2, 79.3; UX-DR106. **Prevents:** a second zone (`note-filter-bar.tsx:253-259`, UX-DR100's band); every wrapped line inset by the glyph lane (`search-field.tsx:171-185`, `:218`); a leading glyph and a clear control of one size (`:301` and `note-filter-bar.tsx:503`, both `size-4`); a permanent reset that loses the text-only clear where it was right; two trailing buttons; a control dropped at 240 px (AD-260, AD-268). **Rule:** UX-DR100 is amended, not deleted: one border encloses a flow that reads *leading glyph → the nine controls → caret/text*, with one stateful trailing action at the first line's end and the chips on the rows after the text. The control order is UX-DR106's: **Add tag** (new, first), Changed by agent, Pinned only, Hide service files, Sort notes, Search drives, Include private notes, New note from search, **Save as space** (a `Save` glyph). Icons wrap greedily in DOM order by UX-DR106's `F / W / A` arithmetic (at 240 px desktop: slots 1–5 then 6–9, caret on physical row 3 at 198 px); the caret joins the last icon row only when ≥ 96 px remain; every continuation line starts at the text inset — 9 px from the field's border-box start — and reserves nothing for a glyph or a clear above it. The leading glyph is 20 px ink, the trailing × is 12 px and the reset arrow 14 px, all in the same 24 / 44 px target, so the two cannot be mistaken. The trailing slot is *Clear search* while no space is entered, or while the entered space's editable projection still equals its `restore`; it is *Reset to «name»* while the projection has drifted (chips, text, flags, origin or sort — the fields `enterSpace` `:401-414` sets; never `vaultIds` or `includePrivate`, which a space does not replace); reset re-applies the restore once and refocuses the text, writes nothing, touches no TTL and parks nothing. The 240 px floor, the ≥ 24 / 44 px targets and AD-268's rule stand; the wrap points and heights are UX-DR106's table and are measured in `dev/probe` (NFR-80). FR-593's "the `[+ tag]` row is gone" still holds — the *control* returns, the *row* does not.

- **AD-289 — Include and exclude are drawn, not spelled.** **Binds:** FR-622, FR-623, FR-636; Stories 79.3, 79.8; UX-DR107. **Prevents:** a sign the colour of its label (`tag-suggest.tsx:73`); an accent-only option row that says nothing about polarity (`:69`); a second vocabulary for the row-tag popover; a font-dependent hyphen for minus. **Rule:** Every signed choice is one of two full-row options per tag, *Include* then *Exclude*: a 24 px sign cell drawing the lucide `Plus` / `Minus`, the label, and a 16 px Check reservation for the polarity the query already holds. The include row wears the include chip's paint (`bg-accent text-accent-foreground`), the exclude row the exclude chip's (`bg-destructive/15 text-destructive`, label-only `line-through decoration-destructive/60`) — the classes at `note-filter-bar.tsx:69-71`, reused, not restated. Choosing the polarity the chip already has is idempotent; choosing the other replaces the term; neither removes — removal is ×. Three entrances share one component: the caret suggestion (`search-field.tsx`, field-width, six rows before scrolling), *Add tag* (280 px, a search input over the vocabulary), and the note-row tag (280 px, that tag's two rows only). `Include tag {tag}` / `Exclude tag {tag}` stay the accessible option names (`:64`).

- **AD-290 — Session switches are switches.** **Binds:** FR-625, FR-626, FR-627; Story 79.4; UX-DR109. **Prevents:** browser-default chrome beside house settings (`note-filter-bar.tsx:388-397`, `space-editor.tsx:540-552`, `space-name-popover.tsx:120-125`, `format-toolbar.tsx:420-425`); a drives picker that acknowledges only its tiny box; a blind swap of a native `<select>` whose empty-string sentinel Radix `Select` refuses (`session-file-actions.tsx:52-55`); a new toggle primitive. **Rule:** Switch (`ui/switch.tsx`) for a single boolean, Checkbox (`ui/checkbox.tsx`) for membership or an affirmative form choice, `RadioGroup` (`ui/radio-group.tsx`) for a small mutually exclusive set; long or dynamic option sets stay selects. Named migrations: `space-editor.tsx` Pinned/Temporary → Switch; `space-name-popover.tsx` Temporary → Switch; `format-toolbar.tsx` "First row is a header" → Checkbox; the drives picker → UX-DR109's row: the whole 48 px row is one `role="checkbox"` named by the drive, `HardDrive` glyph, name over root path, checked fill and Check, stays open after a choice. Select conversions in this epic's surface: *Direction* in both space editors (two options, worded per key) and *Opens* in the session editor (three) → `RadioGroup`; *Sort by* (six keys), templates, files, folders and the task-board status stay native. Epic 80 applies the same rule to `task-form.tsx`.

- **AD-291 — A temporary space is the default offer.** **Binds:** FR-634; Story 79.6; UX-DR108, UX-DR109. **Prevents:** a naming popover whose default writes a permanent file for what is usually a working set (`space-name-popover.tsx:35`); a default that rewrites existing spaces. **Rule:** The naming popover opens with *Temporary space* ON and 48 h prefilled (`:36` already); switching it off makes a permanent draft and changes nothing else. A search someone bothered to save is far more often a working set than a permanent space, and a temporary space is one switch away from permanent. The editor of an existing space shows the space's actual lifetime; *Duplicate* copies the source's; the default is for new drafts only. The spine's "(unchecked for ordinary save)" is revoked.

- **AD-292 — A space is stated once.** **Binds:** FR-618; Story 79.1; UX-DR107. **Prevents:** the scope chip and the field showing one sentence twice (`notes-pane.tsx:312` quotes the prompt into the name; `notes-filters.ts:408` restores the same prompt into the text); a scope chip indistinguishable from an include chip (`note-filter-bar.tsx:511` vs `:71`); a rename of the owner's files to fix a presentation. **Rule:** The scope chip is a third treatment — `bg-muted`, `text-foreground`, 1 px `border`, 7 px rounded rectangle, a 16 px `Folder` glyph and a visible `Space:` prefix, only its × actionable (`Clear scope {full name}`); tag and flag chips keep their pills. When the space's stored name is exactly the restored prompt (ignoring the auto-name's quote wrapper), the chip's visible label reads `Space` and the real name and drive stay in its description and hint — the prompt then appears only in the text region. No file is renamed and no DSL is parsed for this rule; the name composition at `notes-pane.tsx:308-314` is unchanged.

- **AD-293 — A tag in a row proposes; it does not act.** **Binds:** FR-636; Story 79.8; UX-DR107. **Prevents:** a click that narrows the list before the person said which way (`note-row.tsx:344-348` → `notes-pane.tsx:738`); a search-result row with no tags to click (`:226` gives hit rows zero); three visible chips that are the ones already in the query. **Rule:** Clicking a row's tag chip — in the row or in its `+n` popover (`:353-394`) — opens AD-289's popover anchored to that chip with that tag's two options; opening or dismissing it filters nothing, selects nothing and opens no note; choosing calls `setTagTerm`. Hit rows show tags too. A row shows up to three tags, stable-partitioned so tags absent from the query come before tags already included or excluded, source order kept within each partition; overflow keeps the `+n` route. `onToggleTag` and its `cycleTag` wiring go.

- **AD-294 — The rail follows the drives the search covers.** **Binds:** FR-628, FR-629, FR-630, NFR-81; Story 79.5; UX-DR108. **Prevents:** a list searching four drives above trees showing one (`notes-pane.tsx:627-635` on `activeVaultId`; `notes_spaces(vault_id)` `notes_ipc.rs:2003`); a TypeScript union of rails; synthetic ids (`keeper:all`, `keeper:temporary`, `keeper:group:*`) colliding across vaults in one flat list; a per-vault hue (DESIGN-NOTES.md:209); a space entered from drive B resolved against drive A's snapshot (`project_list` reads the lens from the command's vault, `notes_ipc.rs:570-575`). **Rule:** `notes_spaces` takes `vault_ids: Vec<String>` and answers `Vec<NoteSpaceDriveVm>` — one group per drive in the vault list's order, each `{ vault_id, name, root, error, rows }` where `rows` is exactly today's `compose_rail` output for that drive with `vault_id` stamped on every `NoteSpaceVm`; an unreadable drive is a group with its `error` sentence and no rows, never a missing group. keeper-core's `rail.rs::compose_drives` composes the groups; the shell only reads. `notes_tag_tree` takes the same set and answers per-drive groups of nodes; `notes_tree` stays per vault (it is lazy per folder) and the physical tree mounts once per selected drive. React sends the effective set — `vaultIds`, or the active vault when it is empty (the `selectedIds` rule at `note-filter-bar.tsx:174`, moved into the store) — and renders one drive with no header, two or more with UX-DR108's muted header rows (`HardDrive`, the name, the root in the hint). ## The failure shape this epic must not repeat

Two, and the second one is this epic's own subject.

**The wiring lives in the shell, which cannot compile on this host.** The pure halves — `flipTagTerm`, `spaceDrift`, `compose_drives`, `NotesConfig::validate`, `projected`'s prefix — are TypeScript or keeper-core and are proved on Linux, mutation by mutation. What makes 79.5 and 79.9 true for the owner is in `notes_ipc.rs` (the multi-drive command, the refusal, `apply_settings`) and `notes_vault.rs` (`parse_note`'s prefix, the cache discard) — and the shell does not build here. Every story touching them says **by inspection, awaits CI's macOS job** in its acceptance and names the hesperia observation that turns the claim into a fact (the two-drive rail, the refusal sentence, the cache discard in the log). A core signature that changes — `compose_rail`, `parse_note`, `projected`, `seed` — is grepped for callers in `crates/keeper/src` in the same change.

**A test re-pinned to the new string.** This epic reverses decisions that five test files pin (`note-filter-bar.test.tsx:221-241`, `notes-filters.test.ts:253-269`, `tag-tree.test.tsx:91-116`, `space-editor.test.tsx:140-153`, `session-space-editor.test.tsx:202-210`), and several more that pin captions, defaults and buttons the owner removed (`space-name-popover.test.tsx:75`, `notes-pane.test.tsx`'s `NEW_NOTE_LABEL` cases, `note-list.test.tsx:145-163`). The risk on the way out is the mirror image of last week's: a test rewritten to click `Exclude tag draft` where it clicked the body, asserting the same deletion by a new route; a `Temporary` assertion swapped for `Temporary spaces` with no case for the icon; a "clears in one activation" test that never enters a space. A test that pins a rendering moves to the contract — the store after the press, the request after the save, the sentence after the refusal — or it goes. And one that only this epic can produce: a chip grammar that flips in the bar and still cycles in the tree, because the store kept `cycleTag` "for the tree"; there is no compatibility path, and the grep for `cycleTag` and `nextTagChipState` after 79.1 must find nothing.
