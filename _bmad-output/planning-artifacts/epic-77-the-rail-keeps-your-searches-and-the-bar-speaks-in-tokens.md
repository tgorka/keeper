## What the triage found

Three of the twenty-three are not what they look like, and the owner must see all three in the PR bodies:

- **The save-as-space defect is a stale rail, not a missing dialog.** The bookmark works: `note-filter-bar.tsx:460-475` → `notes-pane.tsx:290-299` composes a name from the chips and calls `saveFilterAsSpace` (`use-notes-actions.ts:213-250`) → `notes_space_save` (`notes_ipc.rs:1983`), and the file lands under `spaces/`. Nothing then tells the rail: `SpaceList` reloads on mount and on vault change only (`space-list.tsx:111-131`, `reload` deps `[vaultId]`), and the only path back into it is the editor's `onSaved` (`:371-374`). From the owner's chair: pressed, nothing appeared, "cannot create anything". The absence of a dialog is deliberate (UX-DR35, `notes-pane.tsx:281-288`) and is narrowed here, not deleted (AD-271).
- **The "black popups" are the inverted tooltip chip plus the OS dark appearance, not a broken palette.** Every popup primitive paints `bg-popover` (dialog, sheet, popover, dropdown, context menu, select, command, sonner); `--popover` is `#eceae2` in light (`index.css:142`) and `#141a17` in dark (`:259`), and both are in the shipped CSS. The one near-black surface on a light UI is the tooltip — `tooltip.tsx:39` `bg-foreground text-background`, shadcn's inverted-chip convention — under every icon button in the app, and `kbd.tsx:8` even carries a retint for it. The dark block is applied only by `next-themes` following the OS (`main.tsx:9`); a simultaneously-light-app / black-popup state is not producible from this build. What *is* broken is the inverse: the capture and voice windows mount without the provider (`capture-main.tsx:88-91`, `voice-main.tsx:50-53`) and stay light when the app goes dark (AD-279).
- **Removing the note-row dot removes the only carrier of read state.** The dot (`note-row.tsx:276-298`) is the read/unread mark: filled for unread, hollow for read, never absent, and the comment at `:276-290` is the record of why hollow beats absent. Unread is double-encoded already — bold (`:301`), the provenance line (`:349-353`), "unread" in the accessible name (`:233`) — but *read* is carried by the hollow dot alone. The owner overrules the dot; the accessible name takes the state over (Story 77.5).

## The one sentence

**A rail that cannot show the space you just saved, cannot hold a search for a while, cannot nest or pin or be right-clicked; a bar that cannot sort, cannot look in two drives, cannot keep a private note private, folds a row to add a tag and needs two clicks to clear; rows that repeat their titles and print link syntax; tooltips read as broken popups; and a Back button hiding at the far end of the header: the rail learns to keep your searches, and the bar learns to speak in tokens.**

## Decisions this epic takes

- **AD-269 — A space's lifetime, pin and parentage are `keeper:` frontmatter keys; keeper-core reads them, the rail never invents them.** **Binds:** FR-580, FR-581, FR-584, NFR-73; Stories 77.2, 77.3; UX-DR103. **Prevents:** two producers of the hierarchy (`IndexEntry::tag_tree`, `index.rs:493`, is the one tree keeper-core already grows from `/`); a rail that sorts differently from the palette or from the phone; a naming rule in TypeScript (the `sort_effective` rule at `vm.rs:355-365`: Rust decides, the form selects what Rust decided); a `keeper.` key read in one place and written in another. **Rule:** Three new keys in the space note's `keeper:` map beside `space`, `sort` and `order` (`notes_ipc.rs:1999-2006`): `pinned: true`, `ttl_hours: <positive integer>`, `expires: <RFC 3339>`; a fourth, `text`, is AD-272's. The name's `/` is the parentage: `Journal/Bali` is the space *Bali* under *Journal*, and `naming::slug_stem` (`naming.rs:81-95`) already folds the slash into the filename. keeper-core owns every rule: a new `notes/lifetime.rs` reads the keys the way `sort::read_order` reads `order` (`sort.rs:84-108`) — a value it cannot read is a warning sentence on the row, never a refusal — and a new `notes/rail.rs::compose_rail` replaces the shell's `compose_space_rows` (`notes_ipc.rs:1750-1795`, which becomes the call; its test at `:6879-6903` moves with it). `NoteSpaceVm` (`vm.rs:338-439`) grows `pinned: bool`, `ttl_hours: Option<u32>`, `expires_ms: Option<i64>`, `expiry_phrase: String`, `text: Option<String>`, `parent: Option<String>` (the parent **row's id**, never a name), `leaf_name: String`, `depth: u32`, `descendants: u32` (file-backed spaces beneath, at any depth; 0 on a leaf — the group count the rail shows). Rows with no file are synthetic, not flags: `keeper:temporary` under `keeper:all`, present only while a temporary space exists; `keeper:group:<full path>` for a missing ancestor (*Journal* when only *Journal/Bali* exists), `query: ""`, `updated_ms: None` — no edit, no delete, no scope, exactly the treatment `keeper:all` and `keeper:uncategorized` get today (`notes_ipc.rs:932-935`, `space-list.tsx:321`), the ids mirrored in TS constants beside `src/lib/notes/all-spaces.ts`. A temporary space never joins the slash tree: its `leaf_name` is its whole name and its parent is the Temporary group, whatever its name contains. Order, computed once in Rust and never re-sorted by TypeScript: *All notes* → *Temporary* (pinned first, then soonest `expires_ms`, then `rail_order`) → the persistent tree depth-first, siblings pinned-then-`sort::rail_order` (`sort.rs:117-121`) → *Uncategorized*. `NoteSpaceReq` (`vm.rs:1259-1293`) gains `pinned`, `ttl_hours`, `text` and does not carry `expires`, which only keeper writes. `INDEX_SCHEMA` (`index.rs:50`) goes 5→6 once for the epic77-core rung so `parse_note` (`notes_vault.rs:1943`) can stamp a `temporary` flag (the `template` shape, `:1963-1977`) on every space carrying `ttl_hours`; the `is:` set (`docs/notes.md:106`) gains `temporary`.

- **AD-270 — A temporary space expires into the vault trash, never into nothing, and its clock is a file fact.** **Binds:** FR-579, FR-580, FR-583, NFR-73; Story 77.2; UX-DR103. **Prevents:** a deletion no history records; a purely local clock that deletes on machine B a space you used on machine A an hour ago; a synced file that takes a commit per click; a reaper that is a second mutator of the vault; a countdown that ticks on every row (research §5: no surveyed product shows one). **Rule:** Expiry runs in the vault reconciler — the single sanctioned mutator (`notes_vault.rs:760-984`) — on the ~1 Hz cadence tick it already receives as `Work::EmbedTick` (`:3284-3289`, AD-62), at most once a minute, over entries flagged `temporary` only, so it reads no file while there are none: a space whose `keeper.expires` is in the past goes through `notes_vault::trash_note` (`:2316`) — the path `notes_delete` takes (`notes_ipc.rs:3256`) — so `.keeper/trash/` holds it, `mark_dirty` (`:3269`) queues the commit, and the change channel reports the removal like any other. Opening a temporary space rewrites `keeper.expires = now + ttl` **only when more than a tenth of its TTL has elapsed** since the stored value (`lifetime::touch_due`: 48 h → at most one write per 4.8 h, 2 h → one per 12 min) through a new read-then-splice command `notes_space_touch`; every other open costs nothing and writes nothing. Default TTL 48 h (`lifetime::DEFAULT_TTL_HOURS`), the parked search's 2 h (`PARK_TTL_HOURS`), any positive integer from the editor. `keeper.expires` is written on create from the TTL, re-carried on every update the way `default` is (`notes_ipc.rs:2069-2071`, because the `keeper:` map is spliced whole, `:2064`), recomputed only when the TTL changed, and dropped together with `ttl_hours`. The row states the horizon in whole days, composed in Rust (`expiry_phrase`: `expires in 2 d`, `expires today`; the exact instant on hover from `expires_ms`) — Arc's contract (research §5): reset on open, an archive stage, nothing finer than a day. A pin never stops the clock.

- **AD-271 — Creating a space asks for its name in an anchored popover, and the rail refreshes from the save.** **Binds:** FR-578, FR-579; Story 77.1; UX-DR104. **Prevents:** a save whose only evidence is a file nobody sees until relaunch (`space-list.tsx:111-131` reloads on `[vaultId]`, and the bar's save has no way back into it, `use-notes-actions.ts:213-250`); a modal in front of a one-keystroke action; a *New space…* that exists only as an editor for spaces that already exist (`space-list.tsx:363-376`). **Rule:** UX-DR35 ("nothing in this phase is a dialog", `notes-pane.tsx:281-288`) is narrowed, not deleted: *Save as space* opens a non-modal `Popover` anchored to its button, one text field prefilled with the name `onSaveAsSpace` composes today (`:290-299`), Enter saves, Escape cancels, no focus trap, the bar underneath keeps working. The rail reloads from a signal, not from a remount: `notesFiltersStore` gains `spacesNonce` / `requestSpacesReload()` (the `searchNonce` idiom, `notes-filters.ts:302,342`), raised by `saveFilterAsSpace` on success, by `SpaceEditor.onSaved` (`space-list.tsx:371-374`), by the delete path (`:387-396`), by the park and touch callers (77.3), and by `useNotesChanges` whenever a batch names a path under `spaces/` — which is what makes an expiry (AD-270) or an Obsidian edit reach the rail too. The rail's context menu (AD-280) gains *New space…*, which opens `SpaceEditor` in a creation mode (`space: null`, an empty draft, saving with `id: null` through the same `notes_space_save`, `notes_ipc.rs:1983`).

- **AD-272 — Selecting a space replaces the whole query; merging is a named verb.** **Binds:** FR-582, FR-583, FR-586; Stories 77.2, 77.3; UX-DR103. **Prevents:** the invisible residue the owner hit (`setScope` replaces only `scope`, `notes-filters.ts:303-306`, so a space is entered with the last search's chips and prompt still narrowing it); two contracts for one act across the rail, the Files tree, the recordings space and the scope chip; a prompt a space cannot keep. **Rule:** `setScope` is deleted and every caller (`space-list.tsx:253,393`, `physical-tree.tsx:118`, `note-filter-bar.tsx:368`, `recordings-space.ts:95`) moves to `enterSpace(scope, restore)`: scope, tag chips, text, `agentOnly`, `pinnedOnly` and the bar's sort override are all replaced by the space's own — `keeper.text` restores the prompt, absent means empty; the space's `sort_effective` is the ordering until the bar overrides it (AD-277). A space may therefore have an empty `space:` query when it has a prompt: `notes_space_save`'s refusal of an empty query (`notes_ipc.rs:1990-1998`) becomes a refusal of *no terms and no prompt*, and the lens for an empty query is the unscoped path *All notes* already uses (`:1764`). `saveFilterAsSpace` writes the prompt to `keeper.text` and no longer folds it into the query as `text:"…"` (`use-notes-actions.ts:227-232`), so a space entered is exactly the search it was saved from; the `text:` predicate itself is untouched (epic 76, "Space `text:` semantics" — a hand-written `text:` space still filters). *Add to current search* is a context-menu verb that merges: scope becomes the space, chips and toggles stay, the prompt stays when non-empty and is otherwise the space's. Leaving a scope that is not a temporary space while chips, toggles or a prompt are set first parks that search as `actual-<scope label>` (AD-270's 2 h TTL, one per label — `notes_space_park` finds and refreshes the existing one rather than adding a sibling). Re-selecting the active space still drops to *All notes* (`sameScope`, `notes-filters.ts:98-109`) — through `enterSpace`, so it clears everything too.

## Coordinator amendments after the review wave (2026-09-20)

Written here because the code shipped with them and a reader of the epic alone would otherwise be told the wrong thing.

- **`depth` is the rail's indentation level, not a count of separators.** All notes = 0; the Temporary group = 1 (parent `keeper:all`); a temporary space = 2; a persistent root = 0; `Journal/Bali` = 1 under a `Journal` row at 0; Uncategorized = 0. Where the story prose said persistent roots sit at depth 1, the prose was wrong.
- **Lifetimes are UTC.** The review found `expires_at` / `should_touch` / `expiry_phrase` called with the date-DSL clock (`local_now_ms`) while the sweep compared true UTC: east of UTC a space outlived its TTL, west of it a freshly parked 2 h search was already expired and was trashed within a minute. Every lifetime call site now uses UTC; `local_now_ms` stays for the DSL.
- **A space restores a sort only when its file names one.** `sort::read("")` answers `DEFAULT_SORT`, so restoring it made every entered space an explicit `modified desc` override that killed relevance ranking and outlived the space. `restore.sort` is `None` for a space with no `sort:` key, and leaving a space clears the override.
- **`includePrivate` and the selected drives are not part of what a space replaces.** Entering a space replaces the query — scope, chips, text, flags, sort — and nothing else. (FR-589's session-scoped privacy wins over the implementation contract's earlier "persisted like hideServiceFiles".)
- **A parked search does not claim notes from Uncategorized.** `uncategorized_query` excludes temporary spaces; otherwise a 2 h parked search hid its own notes from Uncategorized until it expired.
- **Expiry does not run on the phone tier.** It rides the reconciler's tick and the phone has no supervisor (Epic 66, NFR-57), so a temporary space made on a phone is reaped the next time a desktop syncs that vault — not never, and not on the phone. Stated rather than silently true.
- **No second clock.** The first attempt at waking a multi-drive search on a secondary vault's change added a `tokio::time::interval`; `src/test/task-host-tick.test.ts` (AD-62) refused it, and the wake is now event-driven off the index watch receivers the subscription already holds.
