# Epic 66 — The rest of the Mac a phone can carry

created: '2026-09-05'
source: the owner, after epic 65 was installed on kalypso — "zaplanuj, zaimplementuj i zainstaluj resztę featerów z mac na ios app". Grounded by two read-only repository maps (every platform gate in Rust and in the frontend), one external research pass (what a Tauri 2 iOS app can reach, September 2026), and one measurement that decides the shape of the whole epic: `cargo check -p keeper-sync --target aarch64-apple-ios` **passes** on hesperia (2026-09-05, 25 s) — the crate was never excluded from the phone because it could not compile, only because nobody linked it.
binds: FR-457…FR-472 (allocated here); NFR-57 (allocated here); AD-197…AD-204 (new); AD-27 (absence, never a dead control — consumed), AD-41 (the git binary is the only process the workspace spawns — consumed, and shown to be the phone's one wall), AD-50 (never force-push a lane — consumed), AD-53 (credentials are programmatic, never a subprocess — consumed), AD-137 (a capability gates its surface — consumed), AD-162 (`botTools` is a linking fact — consumed and inverted), NFR-11 (zero telemetry), NFR-26 (no secret in a diagnostic); collects DW-108 (the phone's dead drawer rows), DW-111 (open-search routes to the desktop dialog), DW-112 ("on this Mac" on the phone), DW-220 (the drive tools cannot reach a phone) and DW-221 (Ollama on the phone).
see-also: epic 56 (virtual files — the pointer-as-placeholder model this epic leans on), epic 62 (the phone tier and the Bots surface without the drive half), epic 65 (the phone that keeps its shape).

## What was measured

1. **Two surfaces need no folder and are dead on the phone.** `sidebar-pane.tsx:79-95` lists Approvals and Bridges unconditionally; the phone drawer renders that sidebar verbatim (`leading-drawer.tsx:121`); a tap sets `primaryView`, and `phone-shell.tsx:268-283` renders only `bots` and `settings` as surfaces — the tap does nothing. `approval-pane.tsx:350-564` already carries the phone idioms (row-tap edit, 44 pt Approve, trailing-swipe discard) that never mount. `bridges-pane.tsx:86-87` gates only the runner on `bridgeSidecar`; discovery, provisioning and health were designed to survive the phone and have no route to it. ⌘3/⌘4 and the palette's `open-approval`/`open-bridges` set views the phone ignores (DW-108). The held-send Delete is hover-only (`conversation-pane.tsx:1631-1640`, no `pointer-coarse` fallback) — a mounted control invisible to a thumb.
2. **Everything else is one absence.** `keeper-sync` is a target-table dependency excluded on iOS and Android (`keeper/Cargo.toml:77-81`); `lib.rs:22-78` gates `sync_ipc`, `notes_ipc`, `notes_vault`, `sessions_ipc`, `copy_ipc`, `bots_drive_ipc` and the four `keeper-*://` asset schemes on `desktop`; `CapabilitiesVm.sync/notes/sessions/botTools` are false on the phone because `git_report` short-circuits to `unsupported` (`ipc.rs:1719-1730`) and `notes_available()` is `cfg!(desktop) && git ok` (`:1539-1541`). Files, Notes, Sessions, Tasks, the drive tools, quick capture, PDF export: all absent by that one construction.
3. **The crate compiles for the phone; the git binary does not exist there.** `git/cli.rs:1-30` is the only module in the workspace that spawns a process, for exactly four things gitoxide lacks: push (upstream #306 closed NOT_PLANNED 2026-07-22), worktree mutation, sparse-checkout patterns, gc. Fetch (`git/fetch.rs`), clone, commit (`git/commit.rs`), fast-forward analysis, the LFS batch client (`lfs/batch.rs`, `lfs/hydrate.rs`) and the pointer model (`lfs/pointer.rs`, `virtual_policy.rs`) are in-process. iOS denies `posix_spawn` to third-party apps (Apple DTS thread 747499): `Command::spawn` returns `Err`, so on the phone every CLI fallback is a refusal, and `filter.lfs.process` can never run — which is fine, because epic 56's virtual files *are* pointer files that stay pointers until materialised through the batch API.
4. **What the OS refuses, by name.** Tray, menu bar, global hotkey, launch-at-login, sidecar processes (`bbctl`, `keeper-rec`), Finder reveal, the in-app updater, ScreenCaptureKit below iOS 27: no iOS concept, or one the free team cannot use. Universal Links need Associated Domains (not on the free team); a share *extension* needs a second App ID and Swift — deferred with the island's own lesson (AD-194: hesperia's Xcode holds no Apple ID, so no new App ID can be minted today). Share *out* (`UIActivityViewController`), PDF (`WKWebView.createPDF`, the same call `pdf_export.rs` makes on macOS), the document picker and the keychain are all reachable from Rust through the `objc2-*` crates already in the tree.

## The one sentence

**The phone was built without the folder — so everything the folder carries is absent, and two surfaces that never needed it are dead rows in a drawer.**

## Decisions this epic takes

- **AD-197 — A surface that needs no folder reaches the phone as itself.** Approvals and Bridges become phone surfaces in the stack (`phone-shell.tsx`'s `surface` union grows), the drawer's rows navigate, the palette's `open-approval`/`open-bridges` and the chords route there, and the Bridges pane on the phone is the desktop's pane without the runner (already gated by `bridgeSidecar`). No row is ever dead (AD-27). The held-send Delete is visible under `pointer: coarse`.
- **AD-198 — `keeper-sync` links on the phone, and what gix cannot do is a sentence, not an absence.** The target table loses its exclusion; `git_report` on iOS answers a phone-shaped report (`engine: "gix"`, no binary, no version floor) and `CapabilitiesVm.sync` is true on the phone. The four CLI verbs are refused on iOS at the `git/cli.rs` boundary with one sentence each — "this is a phone; keeper pushes/checks out/prunes with its own engine here" — and the callers that need them either take the in-process route (checkout by gix, push by AD-202) or say why they cannot (worktree mutation, gc). No watcher on the phone: `watch.rs` is desktop's; the phone syncs on open, on foreground and on pull-to-refresh, because a battery is not a wall socket (NFR-57).
- **AD-199 — The phone mirrors a profile the Mac already syncs; the remote is the hub, never the Mac.** A profile on the phone is a remote URL plus a credential in the keychain (the same `sync_set_credential`, `kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`), cloned by gix into the app container (excluded from backup), **fully virtual by default** — every LFS file is a pointer until opened — fetched and fast-forwarded by gix. A history the phone cannot fast-forward is named ("the phone has N commits the remote does not; push them or reset the phone's copy") and never merged: there is no merge on the phone, and DW-220's inverted route (the Mac serving the phone) is refused for the same reason it was deferred — it would make the Mac a server with its own threat model.
- **AD-200 — Files and Notes on the phone are the desktop's readers in the phone's stack, and share-out is the phone's reveal.** Browse, open text/markdown/media/PDF through `keeper-file://` and `keeper-note://` (registered on every target), materialise a pointer on open with the batch client, and hand a file to `UIActivityViewController` where the desktop reveals in Finder (`revealInFileManager` stays false; a new `shareOut` capability is true on the phone). Notes: list, search, read rendered, edit in the same editor; quick capture is a sheet on the phone, not a window.
- **AD-201 — Sessions and Tasks stay on the Mac and say so.** Tasks run user commands (AD-C7) and iOS spawns nothing; the sessions board reads a synced folder and could follow the notes reader, but it is forty commands and its own epic (DW row). The disclosure names both.
- **AD-202 — Writing from the phone is a push keeper does itself.** `keeper-sync` gains `git::push_http`: `GET …/info/refs?service=git-receive-pack`, the capability line, `report-status` and `side-band-64k`, one `old new refname` update per lane, and a pack of the objects reachable from `new` and not from `old` written by `gix-pack`, sent as one `POST …/git-receive-pack` with the credential injected programmatically (AD-53) and never a force (AD-50; the update is refused client-side unless `old` is the remote's current tip). The server's `report-status` lines are the result; `ng` lines are shown as the server wrote them, scrubbed (NFR-26). Desktop keeps `git push` (AD-41) — this is the phone's engine, tested on Linux against `git receive-pack --stateless-rpc` behind a local HTTP server, and on the phone against the owner's real remote.
- **AD-203 — What the OS refuses is named once and never built around.** Tray, menu bar, hotkey, launch-at-login, sidecars, Finder reveal, updater, screen recording: `CapabilitiesVm` false, absent surfaces, one line each in the disclosure. Microphone-only recording on the phone is a different feature (an audio memo), not the Mac's screen recording — a DW row, not a story.
- **AD-204 — The disclosure moves with the truth.** `IOS_DISCLOSURE_LINES`, `docs/ios.md` Limitations and `docs/egress.md` are rewritten together: the phone now contacts the sync remote host and its LFS endpoint (a destination class `egress.md` already lists, now from a second device), nothing else new.

## Stories

### 66.1 — Approvals, Bridges and the thumb
AD-197. Phone surfaces for Approvals and Bridges; drawer rows and palette actions route; ⌘3/⌘4 route or are not offered on the phone; the held-send Delete under `pointer: coarse`; DW-111 (`open-search` opens the phone's own search surface) and DW-112 (the search header names the device it runs on) closed.
**Acceptance:** tapping Approvals in the drawer shows the ApprovalPane in the phone stack with its swipe-discard and 44 pt Approve reachable (component tests through the phone shell); Bridges shows discovery/provisioning/health and no runner; no `SidebarView` row on the phone sets a view the shell ignores (a test enumerates the registry against the phone surfaces); the held-send Delete has `pointer-coarse:opacity-100`; DW-108, DW-111, DW-112 closed with `path:line`.
**binds:** FR-457, FR-458, FR-459, AD-197

### 66.2 — The folder on the phone
AD-198, AD-199. `keeper-sync` linked on iOS; the phone-shaped `git_report`; the CLI boundary refuses on iOS with a sentence; clone, fetch and fast-forward checkout by gix with a full-virtual policy; the profile sheet on the phone (remote URL, credential, name); sync on open/foreground/pull-to-refresh; no watcher; `CapabilitiesVm.sync` true on the phone; `keeper-file://` and `keeper-note://` registered everywhere; the container excluded from backup; the divergence sentence.
**Acceptance:** the iOS target compiles with `keeper-sync` linked (hesperia); a profile created on the phone clones the owner's real remote into the container and `sync_browse` lists it; every LFS entry is a pointer (`virtual`) after clone; pull-to-refresh fetches and fast-forwards (Rust test with two bare repos and a gix fetch; hardware run on kalypso); a phone with a local commit the remote lacks shows the divergence sentence and never merges; `git_report.engine == "gix"` on iOS and the desktop report is unchanged; no `Command::new` executes on iOS (the boundary test asserts the refusal sentence under a cfg-shaped fake).
**binds:** FR-460, FR-461, FR-462, FR-463, NFR-57, AD-198, AD-199

### 66.3 — Files on the phone
AD-200. The Files pane as a phone surface: browse, open text/markdown/media/PDF, materialise on open, share out through `UIActivityViewController` (`shareOut` capability; `objc2-ui-kit` from `view_controller()`), pull-to-refresh, the offline pill; no reveal, no drag, no fixed widths beyond 390 pt.
**Acceptance:** on kalypso the owner's synced folder browses and a markdown, an image and a PDF open; a pointer materialises on first open and reads on the second without a fetch; the share sheet opens for a file; component tests through the phone shell at 390 pt with no horizontal overflow (the measure rig at 430×932).
**binds:** FR-464, FR-465, FR-466, AD-200

### 66.4 — Notes and capture on the phone
AD-200, AD-202. Notes list, search and rendered read on the phone; the editor; quick capture as a sheet; a save is a commit (gix) and a push (66.5) — until the push lands, a save commits locally and the divergence sentence from 66.2 says so.
**Acceptance:** notes list and search on kalypso against the owner's vault; a note opens rendered and edits in the editor; capture creates a note; a save produces a commit visible in `git log` of the phone's container copy; with 66.5 landed the commit is on the remote within the same run; `CapabilitiesVm.notes` is `sync` on every target (the desktop gate dropped, the git-ok gate kept on the desktop).
**binds:** FR-467, FR-468, FR-469, AD-200

### 66.5 — A push keeper does itself
AD-202. `git::push_http` in `keeper-sync`: discovery, capabilities, `report-status`, `side-band-64k`, the pack from `gix-pack`, the fast-forward guard, the credential callback, scrubbing; the phone's sync uses it after every commit; the desktop does not.
**Acceptance:** a Rust test pushes one commit and one LFS pointer commit to a bare repo behind `git receive-pack --stateless-rpc` on a local HTTP server and reads `ok refs/heads/<lane>` back; a non-fast-forward is refused client-side before any bytes are sent; a server `ng` line reaches the caller scrubbed; on kalypso a capture from 66.4 appears on the owner's remote and on the Mac after its next sync. If the owner's remote refuses the push for a reason this story cannot fix in its budget, the exact server line is a DW row and the phone stays a reader that says so — measured, not assumed.
**binds:** FR-470, FR-471, AD-202

### 66.6 — The disclosure, the docs and the gates
AD-201, AD-203, AD-204. `IOS_DISCLOSURE_LINES` and `docs/ios.md` Limitations rewritten together (the byte-identical guard holds); `docs/egress.md` (the phone's destinations); `docs/decisions.md` D-15 (the folder on the phone) and D-16 (the push); DW-108/111/112/220/221 closed or re-scoped; new DW rows (sessions on the phone, audio memo, share-in extension, tablet tier untouched); every gate including the macOS one and the phone install.
**binds:** FR-472, AD-197…AD-204

## What stays out
- The sessions board and the tasks runner on the phone (AD-201).
- A share extension, Universal Links, push notifications: each needs a second App ID or the paid team (D-1); the island already measured the first wall.
- Screen recording on the phone, the sidecar's screen+audio model; an audio memo is a later feature.
- A merge on the phone. Divergence is named; the Mac merges.
- The Mac as the phone's server (DW-220's inverted route).

## The failure shape this epic must not repeat
Epic 62 built the Bots surface on the phone with its drive half absent "by construction" and wrote that the construction was a linking fact — and the fact turned out to be a choice nobody had measured. Every "cannot" in this epic is either an OS refusal quoted from Apple, or a measurement on hesperia or kalypso with the command that produced it. 66.2 and 66.5 end with a run on the phone against the owner's real remote; 66.1 and 66.3 end with a picture at 430×932.
