# Owed device runs — epics 95–99, and epic 109's keeper rows

Every check epics 95–99 owe on a real device or host, in one list. Nothing here has been run: each
row is a claim the build could not prove from the Linux container, CI or delectra's test Synapse,
and each closes only when the operator (or a lane on that host) runs it and records the result where
the row says. Collected on 2026-10-13 from the epics' *As built* "owed" lines, *Owed when built* and
*Operator actions* sections, the lanes' reports and the deferred-work ledger; no row is invented, and
each cites its source. Epics 89–94 owe runs too; they are listed in
`_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/agents-program-89-94-retro-2026-10-13.md` and their epic docs.

83 runs: hesperia 25, kalypso 23, electra 16, the Android tablet 12, a NanoKVM 4, delectra 3.
By epic: 95 — 12, 96 — 23, 97 — 8, 98 — 29, 99 — 11.

**Epic 108 withdrew 41 of them.** Their subjects left keeper when the agents moved to keeper-hq
(108.2, 108.3; `docs/agents.md` § *What left keeper with the agents' move*): the drive host, its
gates and stewards, the Mac's agent settings, and the screen and KVM tools. The push, decision,
board and voice runs stay owed, rewritten for the agents server: a device pairs with it, push
targets and decisions are its rows, and the old Matrix pusher is only checked gone.
Each such row says *Withdrawn* and why; it stays only as the record of what was owed, and nothing
runs it. What keeper-hq's runtime still owes on electra is keeper-hq's own list
(`docs/electra-runs.md` there).

How to read a row:
- **id** is `<epic>-<n>` (epic 98 keeps its rungs' K/H/A/E letters: kalypso, hesperia, Android, electra). A
  prerequisite naming another id means that run comes first.
- **source** `epic-NN:<line>` is a line of `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epic-NN-*.md` as of
  `agents-99-ui` `c966a4de`; `report-*` and `review-*` are the lanes' reports under
  `/tmp/agents-salvage/` (outside the repo); `DW-n` is `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/deferred-work.md`.
- CI checks are not device runs: CI's macOS, iOS and Android jobs compiled stack #486 green (the first
  iOS-shell compile on #492–#497, the first Android build on #498), so the epics' "awaits CI" lines are met.

Where to start: the runs that need no new purchase or enrolment — 98-K1, 98-H2, 98-K10…98-K12 and 98-K14 on
today's free Apple team, and 95-07…95-10 on hesperia — then the turn models (§ *Before any voice run*) for 97,
and the paid Apple team, Sygnal and ntfy for push.
